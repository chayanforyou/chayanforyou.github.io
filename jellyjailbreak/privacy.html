Privacy Policy

Effective date: 19 October 2026

  • We do not ask for your name, email, contacts, location, photos or microphone.

1. Who we are

Jelly Jailbreak: Puzzle Escape ("the app") is a puzzle game made by Chayan Mistry ("we", "us"). This policy explains what information the app uses, why, and the choices you have. It applies to every version of the app, on any platform where it is offered.

2. Information stored on your device

The app saves the following on your device so the game can remember where you are:

  • Levels completed, with stars, scores and moves used
  • Daily puzzle results, streaks and claimed rewards
  • Diamonds, lives, boosters and unlocked or equipped jelly skins
  • Settings such as sound, music and vibration, and whether you have finished the tutorial

This information stays on your device and is not sent to us. If you have turned on your device's own backup, the operating system may include it in that backup to your account.

Items you have bought for real money, such as Remove Ads or the Jelly Pass, are not stored by the app. The app checks them with the app store each time it starts.

3. Advertising

The app is free and shows ads through Google AdMob (the Google Mobile Ads SDK). Some ads are videos you choose to watch for a reward, such as an extra life or a second chance at a level. The app may also show a full-screen ad between levels.

To show and measure ads, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may collect:

  • Your device's advertising identifier, where your device allows it
  • Device information, such as model, operating system and language
  • Your IP address, used to estimate your general location (country or city)
  • How you interact with ads, and diagnostic information about ad loading and crashes

This data is collected and used by Google, which may use it to personalise ads, measure ad performance and prevent fraud. See How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, the app asks for your consent before ads are loaded. You can change your choice at any time in Settings → Privacy options inside the app.

On iPhone and iPad, the app does not ask for permission to track you, so your advertising identifier is not shared and ads there are not personalised with it.

Your choices

  • Limit ad tracking, opt out of personalised ads, or reset or delete your advertising identifier in your device's privacy or ads settings.
  • Buy "Remove Ads" or subscribe to the "Jelly Pass" in the shop to turn off the ads between levels. Optional reward videos stay available, because you choose to watch them.

4. Purchases

In-app purchases and subscriptions are processed by the app store you downloaded the app from (Google Play or the App Store). The store handles your payment details; we never receive them. The app receives a confirmation of each purchase so it can give you what you bought and restore it later. You can manage or cancel the Jelly Pass in your store account. The store's own privacy policy covers how it handles payments.

5. Device access

The app uses:

  • Vibration, for gentle feedback while you play. You can turn it off in Settings.
  • Internet access, used only by ads and purchases. The game itself plays offline.

The app does not access your location, contacts, camera, microphone or files.

7. Keeping and deleting your data

  • On your device: uninstall the app, or clear its data in your device's settings. This deletes your game progress. Purchases are kept by the app store, so tap Restore purchases in the shop after reinstalling to get them back.
  • Advertising data: reset or delete your advertising identifier as described above. Data held by Google is kept under Google's own retention rules.
  • Purchase records: these are kept by the app store under its own rules.

If you want help, email us and we will tell you how.

8. Children

The app is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the app, contact us and we will help remove it.

9. Changes to this policy

If the app starts using information in a new way, we will update this page and change the effective date at the top.
