2. Frequently asked questions

How do I use it?

Hold your phone with the speaker facing down, turn the volume up and tap Clean. Splashout plays tones that shake the speaker, which helps water drip out and dust come loose. Use Quick for a light splash and Deep after a swim or a drop in the sink. The Dust tab loosens dry dust from the speaker grille.

My phone fell in water. Will this fix it?

It helps with small amounts of water stuck in the speaker grille, and with muffled sound afterwards. It cannot repair a damaged phone. If your phone was fully underwater, switch it off, let it dry and do not charge it while it is wet.

I can't hear the tone, or it is very quiet.

Turn the volume up (the app can set it to maximum for you), unplug headphones or disconnect Bluetooth audio, and make sure nothing covers the speaker. Low tones of about 100–300 Hz are faint on small speakers, which is normal. The vibration assist helps you feel that a clean is running.

The clean stopped when I connected headphones.

This is on purpose. Cleaning tones are loud and are meant for the phone's own speaker, so a clean stops when headphones or Bluetooth audio connect. Disconnect them and start again. Earbud Clean (Pro) is the exception: it plays through connected earbuds.

What do the speaker clarity scores mean?

The Speaker Test plays a short sweep and listens with the microphone to estimate how clear the speaker sounds. Test in a quiet room with the phone on a table. The number is only for comparing before and after a clean on the same phone. It is not comparable between different phones.

Why does the app ask for the microphone?

Only the Speaker Test and the Mic Test use it. The audio is processed in memory on your device and is never saved or sent anywhere. All cleaning modes work without microphone permission.

Does it keep running when I lock the phone?

Yes. A running clean keeps playing in the background. On iPhone, a Live Activity on the lock screen and in the Dynamic Island shows the countdown and a Stop button. On Android an ongoing notification does the same. You also get a notification when a clean finishes while you are away.

What does Pro include, and how do I restore it?

Pro is a one-time purchase, not a subscription. It unlocks Earbud Clean and the tone generator's auto-stop timer and extra waveforms, and it removes all ads. To restore it on a new phone, open Settings → Restore purchases while signed in with the same Google or Apple account.

How do I get a refund?

Purchases are handled by the store, not by me. On Google Play, request a refund from your order history. On the App Store, use reportaproblem.apple.com.

What data does the app collect?

The app itself collects nothing: no accounts, no analytics and no crash reporting. The free version shows ads through Google AdMob, which may use your advertising identifier. See the privacy policy for details.
