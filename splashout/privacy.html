  • There are no accounts, no analytics and no third-party crash reporting.

1. Who we are

Splashout ("Splashout: Speaker Cleaner", "the app") is a speaker and earpiece utility made by Chayan Mistry ("we", "us"). This policy explains what information the app uses, why, and the choices you have. It applies to both the Android and iOS versions of the app.

2. Information stored on your device

The app saves your preferences on your device only, so it remembers how you set it up:

  • Theme choice (light or dark), vibration assist and haptic feedback preferences
  • Your last selected cleaning mode (Quick or Deep, Speaker, Earpiece or Earbuds) and active tab
  • Whether the optional dust reminder notification is turned on
  • Local counters and timestamps used to space out full-screen ads and the one-time in-app review prompt

This information stays on your device and is not sent to us. If you have turned on your device's system backup, your settings may be included in that backup to your Google or Apple account.

3. Microphone and audio

Water and dust cleaning, the Stereo Test and the Tone Generator play synthesized sound only and never use the microphone.

The microphone is used only when you choose to run the Speaker Test or the Mic Test:

  • Speaker Test: plays a 3-second test sweep and listens through the microphone to estimate speaker clarity on your device. As soon as the score is calculated, the audio samples are discarded.
  • Mic Test: records up to 10 seconds of audio in memory so you can see the input level and play it back to check your microphone. The recording is discarded as soon as you leave the screen, close the app or tap Record again.

No audio from your microphone is ever written to permanent storage or transmitted off your device. You can use all cleaning modes without granting microphone permission.

4. Advertising

The free version of the app shows ads through Google AdMob (the Google Mobile Ads SDK). To show and measure ads, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may collect:

  • Your device's advertising identifier, where your device allows it
  • Device information, such as model, operating system version, screen size and language
  • Your IP address, used to estimate your general location (country or city)
  • How you interact with ads, and diagnostic information about ad loading and performance

This data is collected and used by Google, which may use it to personalise ads, measure ad performance and prevent fraud. See How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, the app asks for your consent (via Google's User Messaging Platform) before ads are loaded. You can change your choice at any time in Settings → Privacy choices inside the app.

On iPhone and iPad, the app does not ask for App Tracking Transparency permission to track you across other companies' apps and websites, so your iOS advertising identifier (IDFA) is not accessed for cross-app tracking.

Your choices

  • Limit ad tracking, opt out of personalised ads, or reset or delete your advertising identifier in your device's privacy or ads settings.
  • Unlock Splashout Pro (a one-time purchase) to remove all ads from the app. Once Pro is active, the app does not initialize or load ads.

5. Purchases

The optional one-time Splashout Pro upgrade is processed by the app store you downloaded the app from (Google Play Billing on Android, or Apple StoreKit on iOS). The store handles your payment details; we never receive your card number, billing address or other payment information.

The app checks your purchase status with the app store so it can unlock Pro features on your device and restore them when you tap Restore purchases. Each store's own privacy policy (Google Privacy Policy, Apple Privacy Policy) covers how it handles payments.

8. Keeping and deleting your data

  • Purchase records: retained by Google Play or Apple under their respective store policies.

9. Children's privacy

Splashout is a general utility and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the app, please contact us and we will help remove it.
